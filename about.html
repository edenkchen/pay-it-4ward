The short version

Still learning, always sharing

Kai picked up pickleball around two years ago and found a deep, engulfing passion for the sport almost immediately. That's not a throwaway line — it's the reason the lessons feel the way they do.

Because he learned the game recently himself, he remembers exactly what it feels like to be confused at the kitchen line, to shank a third shot, to wonder why the ball keeps popping up. So instead of drowning students in jargon, he breaks things down into pieces that click.

Off the court, you'll usually find him outdoors — fishing or hiking. On the court, he's the coach cheering the loudest when something finally clicks for you.

It's also why he doesn't charge. The game was handed to him by people who had no reason to bother, and coaching for free is how he keeps that going. The only thing he asks is that you pay it forward to the next person.
