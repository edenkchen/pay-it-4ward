The approach

Coaching that actually sticks

Every session is built around what you want to fix.

🎯

Meet you where you are

No cookie-cutter drills. Kai reads your game first, then builds the lesson around your level and the goals you actually care about.

🧠

Understand the why

You won't just be told what to do — you'll learn the reasoning behind each shot so you can make better decisions mid-rally.
