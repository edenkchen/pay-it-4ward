❓ Good to know

Common questions

I've literally never played. Is that okay?

More than okay. The Intro session exists exactly for this. You'll learn the rules, how to score, and what the kitchen is, and you'll be rallying by the end of it.

What should I bring?

Court shoes, water, and comfortable clothes. If you don't own a paddle yet, mention it when you book and Kai will bring a loaner.

Where do lessons take place?

Court location depends on availability and where you're based. Text Kai and he'll sort out the most convenient spot.

Can I book with a friend?

Absolutely — semi-private sessions for two or three players are one of the most popular options, and you split the cost.

How do I pay and how do I cancel?

Payment details are arranged when you book. For cancellations, give as much notice as you can so the court slot can be reused.

Not sure which to pick?

Send a quick note about where your game is at and Kai will point you to the right starting place.
