How this works

Coaching costs nothing.

There's no rate card, no session fee, no package to buy. Kai coaches because the sport gave him something and he'd rather keep that moving than charge for it.

What he asks instead is in the name: pay it forward. Give something back to the pickleball community, or chip in toward the gear that keeps these sessions running. How you do that is genuinely up to you — and nobody checks.

❓ Good to know

Common questions

Wait — it's actually free?

Yes. No session fee, no package, no card on file. Kai isn't running this as a business.

Then what am I actually expected to give?

Something back to the sport, in whatever form suits you — time, help, gear, or money. The section above lists ideas. Nobody keeps score, and there's no minimum. The one thing Kai asks is that you don't just take and disappear.

Do I have to take the assessment?

For an individual lesson it's how Kai figures out where to start, so it saves everyone time. It's five multiple-choice questions and nothing is sent anywhere until you hit send on the contact form. If you'd rather skip it, just message him and say so.

Is the level it gives me an official rating?

No. Real ratings like DUPR or a USA Pickleball rating come from played matches. This is a self-assessment that puts you in a rough band so the first lesson starts in the right place — Kai adjusts it once he sees you play.

I've literally never played. Is that okay?

Completely. Answer the first question honestly and the whole lesson gets built around that. You'll learn the rules, how to score, what the kitchen is, and you'll be rallying by the end.

What should I bring?

Court shoes, water, comfortable clothes. If you don't own a paddle yet, say so when you book and Kai will bring a loaner.

Where do lessons take place?

It depends on court availability and where you're based. Message Kai and he'll sort out the most convenient spot.

Can I book with a friend?

That's exactly what the small group option is for — two or three players, same session.
